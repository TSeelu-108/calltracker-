What the planner uses

Plan my week builds your route from these four things. The more you add, the better the plan.

1Who you need to see

Your remaining calls, from the Call Tracker.

2Where and when they're in

Your best-times spreadsheet: each doctor's office and the days they're there.

3Who your counterparts are seeing

Their schedules for this week, so you never see the same doctor on the same day.

4What's already on your calendar

An export from Outlook (.ics file). Meetings and time off stay free, and your lunches are planned in.
